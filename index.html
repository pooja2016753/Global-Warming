Global Warming

Global warming is the long-term heating of Earth's climate system. It is caused by human activities such as burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial processes, which release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere.

Global warming poses significant risks to our environment and society. It leads to rising sea levels, extreme weather events, loss of biodiversity, and disruptions to ecosystems.

The impacts of global warming are felt worldwide, affecting communities, economies, and ecosystems across the globe. It is imperative that we take urgent action to mitigate climate change and adapt to its effects.
